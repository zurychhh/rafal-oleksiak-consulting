Grid, order and the fold

Eight-point grid. Outer margins 88 px on desktop. Two columns: the drawing on the left, the argument on the right. Every block stands on the same grid and the same margins — top bar, both columns, the position blocks, the exit bar.

DOM order is the phone order. Desktop composes two columns out of it with a named-areas grid — never the other way round. This came out of a measurement, not taste: with desktop order the amber exit bar landed below four service blocks, under the fold. After the change the CTA starts at 539 px against a 844 px fold in Safari on iOS.

390 is a separate decision, not a squeezed desktop. Above the fold: the claim, the service line and the way out.
